The fine print

Terms of Use

Effective 24 October 2026

There is no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive people in Witbound. Anything others can see can be reported, people can be blocked, and anyone who breaks these terms is removed.

Who these terms are for

These terms apply to everyone who uses Witbound on iPhone, iPad, Mac or Android, and in particular to its community features: community sync, the shared Synced list and the Roadmap in Discover (and the Wanted list, in apps that still show it), and Clubs, where friends read the same book on a shared schedule. If you got Witbound from the App Store, Apple’s Standard End User License Agreement also applies. If you got it from Google Play, Google Play’s terms apply as well.

Your books

Use Witbound only with books and audiobooks you have the right to use. Witbound does not provide, host or link to books, and it never removes copy protection.

What others can see

In Clubs, the other members of a club see your Clubs name (a name you choose, with a 4-digit tag), the picture you pick, if any, from the 24 engravings that come with the app, the names of the clubs you are in, the books you add or suggest, and your progress in the club’s current book: chapter, percent, stage, status, when you last read and whether you are reading now. They also see the quotes you share from the club’s book, each shown from their own copy once they reach its chapter, and how many of each reaction a quote has. Your note on a highlight is never shared. Your ebook files never leave your device. On Android, iPhone and iPad, Cloud sync, if you turn it on, sends an audiobook’s audio to Witbound’s server to sync it, and the audio is deleted straight after. In Discover, books you choose to share appear on the public lists without your name. On the Roadmap, ideas you post are public under your Clubs name, and votes are anonymous.

Names, book titles and Roadmap ideas are checked by a word filter and an automated safety check before others see them. A quote carries no text of its own: each member’s app shows the passage from its own copy of the book. Reactions are four fixed kinds. Apart from Roadmap ideas, there is no chat and no free text.

No objectionable content, no abuse

Do not use a name, a club name, a book you add or suggest, a quote or a Roadmap idea to share:

  • sexual content;
  • hateful content, or content that attacks people for who they are;
  • harassment, threats, bullying or impersonation of someone else;
  • spam, advertising or links;
  • anything illegal, or that promotes violence or self-harm.

Do not misuse the service either: no automated access, no attempts to get around limits, bans or blocks, and no interfering with other readers’ progress.

By posting an idea on the Roadmap you agree that it is shown publicly under your Clubs name, and that we may use it in Witbound without payment or credit. Ideas follow the same rules as Clubs: no objectionable content, spam or links. We may remove any idea, or stop any profile from posting.

Report, block and leave

In a club, the menu next to a reader’s name has Report and Block, and the club’s own menu has Report club and Leave club. A report sends the reason you pick; a club or reader that several people report is hidden until we review it. Blocking hides that reader’s content from you, removes them from the clubs you run, and stops them joining any club you run. You can leave a club at any time. You can Unshare a quote you shared, and the club’s owner can remove any quote. Reporting or blocking a reader covers their quotes too. In Discover, any book on the public lists can be hidden or reported. Any idea on the Roadmap can be reported, and one report hides it until we review it. You can block the person who posted it, and delete your own ideas at any time.

We review reports and act on them as quickly as we can, aiming for within 24 hours: we remove objectionable content, and we remove people who post it or who abuse others, which can include banning their device from Clubs. You can also email us at zarksided@gmail.com.

Read or die

A club’s owner can set its schedule to Read or die. In a Read or die club, a member who hasn’t reached the end of a stage by its deadline is removed from the club, and can’t rejoin it with its code until the club starts its next book, unless the owner lets them back in. A deadline that was already set when you joined can’t remove you, nor can one set while you had left the club, and only reading done after you joined, and after the club started the book, counts toward a stage. Leaving before a deadline and rejoining after it doesn’t avoid it. A member whose version of Witbound doesn’t show Read or die is never removed by it, nor is one whose version began showing it less than a day before the deadline. The owner is held to the same rule: if the owner is removed, the club passes to the member furthest along. If nobody reaches the stage in time, nobody is removed.

Deleting your club profile

Delete my club profile, in Settings, removes your name, your place in every club, your suggestions, your quotes and reactions, your club moments and your Roadmap ideas from Witbound’s service. A club you run passes to its longest-standing member, or is deleted if you are its only member. The privacy policy describes what is stored and for how long.

The service

Witbound and its community features are provided as they are, and they may change or stop. We are not responsible for what other readers share, though we act on reports as described above. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have under the law where you live.

Changes

We will update this page when the rules change, and show the effective date above.
