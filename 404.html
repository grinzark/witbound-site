How to join

  1. i.

    Get Witbound from the App Store. Groups is on iPhone and iPad.

  2. ii.

    Open Witbound and tap Groups in the tab bar (on older versions, the Groups icon at the top of your library).

  3. iii.

    Under Join a group, type the code (or paste this link) and join.
