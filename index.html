If your mind wanders during audiobooks, it isn't your fault

Listening alone gives your eyes nothing to do. You drift, and surface four minutes later with no idea what happened.

Reading along fixes it — the words hold your attention while the narrator carries the pace. The problem has always been that the two halves live in different apps and drift out of step within a page.

Witbound puts them back together. It listens to your audiobook once, matches every spoken word to the word on the page, and from then on they move as one.

The spoken word is highlighted as you hear it. Tap any word to send the narration there.

How it works

  1. Bring your own files An ebook and its audiobook — epub or PDF, m4b or mp3. Books you already own, from wherever you got them.
  2. Your device does the matching It transcribes the narration and lines it up with the text. This takes a while the first time, it happens on your device, and it never happens again for that book.
  3. Read and listen as one The highlight follows the voice. Your place is kept, and so is every passage you save.

Made for the big screen too

On iPad the text holds one readable column, in any orientation. A bigger screen means wider margins, not longer lines.

Nothing leaves your device

There is no account, no sign-up and no cloud. Your books are never uploaded, because there is nowhere to upload them to — the matching runs on the device in your hand. Witbound's App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, and that is the whole of it.

Questions

Will it work with my Kindle or Audible books?

No — and it never will. Those files are copy-protected, and breaking that protection is illegal. Witbound refuses them on purpose rather than failing strangely later. Anything DRM-free works: Libro.fm, Downpour, LibriVox, Standard Ebooks, Project Gutenberg, or your own CD rips. New to this? Start with our free public-domain audiobook + ebook pairs.

How long does the first sync take?

Longer than you would like — it depends on the length of the book and the device. You can start reading the moment the first chapter is matched, while the rest catches up behind you.

Do I need an internet connection?

Once, so the device can fetch Apple's speech engine the first time. After that Witbound works entirely offline.

What if the ebook and audiobook don't match exactly?

Different editions, abridgements and missing chapters are normal. Witbound matches what it can and leaves the rest as ordinary unnarrated text, rather than forcing a bad alignment.

Is there a Mac version?

Not yet. It is the most likely thing to come next.
