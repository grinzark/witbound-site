Questions

Good to know.

Will it work with my Kindle or Audible books?

No — and it never will. Those files are copy-protected, and breaking that protection is illegal. Witbound refuses them on purpose rather than failing strangely later. Anything DRM-free works: Libro.fm, Downpour, LibriVox, Standard Ebooks, Project Gutenberg, or your own CD rips. New to this? Start with our free public-domain audiobook + ebook pairs.

How long does the first sync take?

You're reading within minutes. Witbound matches the first chapter first and opens the book right away, while the rest finishes quietly in the background — even overnight while charging. It happens once per book, ever; after that, playback is instant.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only briefly. Reading and listening work fully offline. Witbound goes online for community sync (so a popular book can start in seconds) and for your own Audiobookshelf server if you connect one, and you can turn community sync off.

What if the ebook and audiobook don't match exactly?

Different editions, abridgements and missing chapters are normal. Witbound matches what it can and leaves the rest as ordinary unnarrated text, rather than forcing a bad alignment.

Is there a Mac version?

Yes — Witbound for Mac is a free companion that syncs a book on your Mac in minutes, then sends it to your phone already synced over your own Wi-Fi. It's signed and notarised by Apple and needs macOS 26 or later. Android is in closed testing now.
