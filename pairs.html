How to read along with a pair

  1. Download the ebook (choose the .epub file) and the audiobook (choose the M4B or MP3s) from the links above.
  2. Open Witbound on your iPhone or iPad and import both — the app matches every spoken word to the page, on the device, once.
  3. Press play. The narrator reads; the words light up. Tap any word to jump the narration there.

Get Witbound on the App Store

A note on LibriVox narration. LibriVox recordings are read by volunteers, so quality varies — some are wonderful, some are rough, and group readings switch narrator by chapter. Listen to a few minutes before you commit to a long book. If a reading isn't for you, most classics have several versions on LibriVox to choose from.
