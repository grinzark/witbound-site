Which files work?

Ebooks: .epub (including Kobo .kepub) and .pdf. Audio: .m4b, .m4a, .mp3, .flac, .wav, .aiff. No ebook? Turn on Audio only and Witbound writes the book from the narration, chapter by chapter.

Why won’t my Audible or Kindle file open?

Audible (.aax) and Kindle-store books are copy-protected, and the law forbids breaking those locks, so Witbound never will. DRM-free sources work beautifully: Libro.fm, LibriVox, Standard Ebooks and Project Gutenberg.

Which servers work?

iPhone and iPad: Audiobookshelf for audiobooks; Plex for audiobooks in your music library; ebook catalogues that speak OPDS, such as Calibre, calibre-web, Kavita, Komga, Grimmory, BookOrbit or COPS; and any KOReader sync server for your place. BookOrbit can also take your highlights and notes, if you switch that on.

Mac: Audiobookshelf, Calibre, Grimmory, BookOrbit and Plex. Android: Audiobookshelf, Calibre and other OPDS catalogues, and Plex.

Other media servers don’t work with Witbound. Plain files always do.

How long does syncing take?

A few minutes per hour of audio, on your iPhone. It runs best while charging. You can leave the app: progress is saved chapter by chapter, and a retry resumes where it stopped. You can start reading as soon as the first chapter is done.

If your iPhone gets warm, Witbound rests for a minute or two, then carries on by itself. It waits longer only if your iPhone is too hot. A book paused for warmth or a low battery has a Sync now button, and progress moves within each part, so a long first part doesn’t sit at 0%.

Popular editions are often ready in seconds through community sync. While Witbound checks a shared sync against your own audio, the book shows how many chapters it has checked. You can start reading those, or choose to sync on your iPhone instead. The free Mac app syncs a long book in minutes and sends it to your phone.

What’s free, and what’s Plus?

Your first imported book is free, synced in full. A sync that fails doesn’t use it up. Witbound Plus is a one-time purchase that unlocks every book. Nothing renews.

Have an older subscription? It keeps working until you cancel it in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Refunds and restoring

Purchases are handled by Apple. Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com. Use Restore purchases on the paywall if Plus doesn’t show up on a new phone.

Bought on Google Play? Refunds go through Google Play, and Restore purchases on the paywall brings Plus back on a new Android phone.

Privacy

No account, no ads, no analytics. Details in the privacy policy.
