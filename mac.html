How it works

1. Drop the files inAn ebook (epub or PDF) and its audiobook (m4b or mp3). Folders work too.
2. Your Mac syncs themIt listens through the narration once and lines every word up with the text. A long book takes minutes, not hours.
3. Send to your phoneYour Mac and phone find each other on the same Wi-Fi. One tap, and the book lands in Witbound already synced.

What leaves your Mac

Your books and audio never leave your computer except to go to your own phone on your own network. The only thing shared more widely, if you leave the option on, is the timing map for that book: numbers, no text, no audio. It means the next person with the same edition syncs instantly. There is an off switch.

Questions

Which files work?

DRM-free only: libro.fm, Downpour, LibriVox, Standard Ebooks, Project Gutenberg, and your own rips. Audible and Kindle files are locked and will not open.

Does my phone need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

Yes, for the one-tap send. You can also AirDrop the same files to your iPhone and Witbound will pick up the sync.

Windows or Linux?

Mac first. A Windows version is in beta. Email hello@witbound.app if that is you.

How much?

Free. Witbound for iPhone and iPad is a one-time purchase; the Mac companion just makes it faster.
