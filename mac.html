Free companion for Mac

Sync a book on your Mac. Send it to your phone, already synced.

The first sync of a book is the slow part, and a phone takes its time. Your Mac does the same work in minutes, then hands the finished book to your iPhone or iPad over your own Wi-Fi. Open Witbound and it is there, ready to read along.

Download for Mac Witbound for iPhone & iPad

Free. Version 1.5.5, for macOS 26 or later on Apple silicon and Intel. Signed and notarised by Apple. You need the Witbound app on your iPhone or iPad to read the synced book.

Three steps, from your Mac to your phone.

  1. 1

    Drop the files in

    An ebook (epub or PDF) and its audiobook (m4b, m4a or mp3). Single files or folders, and audiobooks in several parts.

  2. 2

    Your Mac syncs them

    It goes through the narration once and lines every word up with the text. A long book takes minutes, not hours.

  3. 3

    Send to your phone

    Your Mac and phone find each other on the same Wi-Fi. Every finished book has a gold Send button: one click, and the book lands in Witbound already synced. With no phone to send to, the button says why.

Bring books from your own library.

Audiobookshelf
Browse your server for the audiobook.
Calibre and OPDS
Find the ebook in Calibre, Grimmory, BookOrbit or any OPDS catalogue, with title and author suggestions. Grimmory can be searched and browsed by author, series or shelf, and you can type an address on its own, like 192.168.1.10:8080.
Plex
Audiobook parts from your Plex library.
Kindle files
DRM-free azw3 and mobi books convert through calibre, if you have it installed.
Books in parts
Joined without re-encoding, and much faster since 1.5. A part that arrives incomplete stops the import and says which one.
Your shelf
Books on an unplugged drive stay on the shelf. Removing a server book moves its files to the Trash.
Dark Mode
Since 1.5.1 the app keeps its light look when your Mac is in Dark Mode, so every button, menu and field stays readable.

Share what you sync. See what readers want.

Your book is ready
When an EPUB book finishes syncing and its timing map is on community sync, a note under it can ask whether to show the community it’s synced. It shows the book as your files name it and the edition and narrator words a Share would send, and nothing goes to the community unless you click Share. It doesn’t ask about a copy Witbound finds already on the Synced list.
Share from the right-click menu
“Show the community it’s synced…” is in the right-click menu of synced EPUB books it can share, older syncs and books synced with community sync off included. A window shows the book and the edition and narrator words before anything is sent.
One book, one entry
A Share goes straight away. Witbound names the book from the community’s own lists first, so an ebook that spells its author another way can join the book already on the Synced list; then from Open Library, if it answers within a few seconds; or else by the book’s own title and author.
Community
Switch between Your books and Community at the top of the window, or with ⌘1 and ⌘2. Wanted shows the books readers want and how many want each one. Synced shows the books already synced and the edition and narration each was synced from; click one to see them exactly as recorded. Witbound doesn’t provide these books.
Settings
Witbound for Mac › Settings (⌘,) holds Community sync, Offer to share books I sync and Check for updates. All three are also at the foot of the window.

What leaves your Mac.

Your books and audio leave your computer only to go to your own phone, on your own network.

If you leave community sync on, Witbound shares the book’s word timings and fingerprints, never its text or its audio, so the next person with the same edition syncs in seconds. There’s an off switch. With it off, a book’s timings go only if you share that book from its right-click menu, and its window tells you first.

A book’s details go to the community only when you click Share. Witbound names the book from the community’s own lists, or from Open Library if it answers quickly, or else by the title and author in your files, and the book, its edition and its narrator go to Witbound’s community service with a random install ID. The Community view reads the public Wanted and Synced lists, and its covers come from the public catalogues that hold them.

The update check only reads GitHub’s public list of releases. The privacy policy has the rest.

Questions

Which files work?

DRM-free only: Libro.fm, LibriVox, Standard Ebooks and Project Gutenberg. Audible and Kindle-store files are locked and will not open.

Does my phone need to be on the same Wi-Fi?

Yes, for the one-click send. It works over VPNs and on Macs with both Ethernet and Wi-Fi. You can also AirDrop the same files to your iPhone and Witbound will pick up the sync.

Windows or Linux?

Mac first. If you would use Witbound on Windows or Linux, email zarksided@gmail.com so I know.

How much?

Free. Witbound for iPhone and iPad is a one-time purchase; the Mac companion just makes it faster.
